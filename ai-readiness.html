Questions

Before you book.

AI Readiness is new for most local businesses. Here are the common questions.

What is AI Readiness?

AI Readiness is CMD’s recurring service for improving how AI systems understand, trust, and represent your business. Each month we audit, prioritize, implement, measure, and repeat.

Is this the same thing as SEO?

Related, but not the same. Traditional SEO focuses on ranking pages and earning clicks. What people call AI SEO usually points at the same practical foundation we work on here: clean structure, schema, service pages, location clarity, proof, and consistent business facts. AI Readiness keeps the focus on whether AI systems can accurately understand, explain, trust, and summarize your business before someone clicks.

Do I need a new website to do this?

No. AI Readiness works with your existing digital presence. Depending on what we find, the monthly work might include new pages, structured data, clearer proof, better profiles, fresher documentation, or making information you already have more explicit and consistent.

Do you guarantee AI will recommend us?

No. No one can honestly guarantee what an AI system will say. The work makes your business easier to parse, understand, and verify, then shows the evidence of what changed.

Where does the scan fit?

The scan happens before you sign up. It gives us enough signal to decide whether AI Readiness is a fit. The product itself is the ongoing monthly service, not a one-time scan report.

How much does this cost?

AI Readiness starts at $750/month. If the business needs significant foundational cleanup before the monthly loop can work, we will call that out separately before anything starts.
